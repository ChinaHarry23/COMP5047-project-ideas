# Start from the official record, then design

> **中文**：[[from-sources_cn]]
> Method: live web search on 12 Sep 2026. Challenges come from DCCEEW, EPBC KTPs, Threatened Species Action Plan, TERN/ANU 2025 report, CSIRO/BoM State of the Climate 2024, and 2025–26 news. **Not** from “what would make a cute MCU demo.”
> Earlier 20-idea list mixed real issues with invented ones (brush-turkey kiln, noisy-miner baffles, glass-strike as *the* Australian crisis). Those are dropped unless a source puts them on a national urgency list.

**Rule for this unit.** A 13-week ESP32 artefact cannot stop climate change, H5N1, or Appin Road. It *can* embody one documented pressure in a named non-human’s terms, with a campus analog, and say what it is **not**.

## 1. What the record says is urgent

### National (drivers, not species)

| Rank of pressure | What sources actually say | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Invasive species | Affect **82%** of listed threatened species. 13 of 21 EPBC Key Threatening Processes are invasive. | [SoE biodiversity threats](https://soe.dcceew.gov.au/biodiversity/pressures/threats-and-key-threatening-processes) |
| Habitat loss / modification | Primary *mechanism* for ~**70%** of threatened taxa; agriculture, urbanisation, transport. East-coast development called out again in 2024. | SoE; [TERN Aus-Env 2024 PDF](https://www.tern.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024_Aus-Env-Report_FINAL-1.pdf) |

| Population collapse | Threatened birds, mammals, plants, frogs, reptiles down **59%** since 2000; frogs **67%**, reptiles **88%**. 39 species added in 2025 → **2,175** listed. | TERN 2025 |
| Marine heat | Longer marine heatwaves; GBR/Ningaloo bleaching; kelp/seagrass; 2024/25 MHWs + SA *Karenia* bloom. | [CSIRO/BoM SOTC 2024](https://www.csiro.au/en/news/All/Articles/2024/November/State-of-the-Climate-2024); [SA algal bloom](https://www.algalbloom.sa.gov.au/) |

**EPBC Key Threatening Processes you can name without inventing a problem** (full list on [SPRAT](https://environment.gov.au/cgi-bin/sprat/public/publicgetkeythreats.pl)):

- Predation by **feral cats** (TAP updated **2024**, jointly made with NSW and others)
- Predation by **European red fox**
- Infection of amphibians with **chytrid** fungus
- Dieback caused by **Phytophthora cinnamomi**
- **Novel biota** (covers **myrtle rust**)
- Cane toads, feral pigs, gamba/fire ants, marine debris, etc.

**Commonwealth 2022–2032 Action Plan** ([110 priority species](https://www.tern.org.au/threatened-species-guidelines/)): 2026 targets include cat/fox management across important habitat, myrtle-rust insurance collections, climate-resilience actions. **Sydney-relevant names on that 110 list:** koala (Qld/NSW/ACT), swift parrot, regent honeyeater, eastern curlew, hooded plover (eastern), White’s seahorse, Wollemi pine, spectacled flying-fox (Qld; grey-headed is EPBC vulnerable but **not** on the 110 — still list it as EPBC, don’t fake the 110 badge).

## 3. Artefacts derived from those sources

Each row: **the issue is cited first**. Interaction design is second. Architecture is a campus analog. Vision Pro = inquiry overlay, never the Pass product.

### A. Cats — KTP + TAP 2024 + NSW roaming debate

**Challenge.** Cats kill over 1.5 billion native animals a year nationally; roaming pet cats add tens of millions in NSW, and NSW still has no enforceable containment.

**Non-human.** Bandicoots, small mammals, reptiles, frogs named in the TAP prey lists — not “a possum we like.”

**IxD.** Fail-closed to cat-class bodies after dusk; fail-**open** for wildlife-sized bodies if the classifier dies. Geometry does more than AI. No ultrasound (hits wildlife). No live-cat tests.

**Architecture.** ESP32-S3-CAM + BH1750 dusk + LD2410 → MG90S gap on a demo fence. Image API = Credit. W2 proxemics. Demo: stuffed cat vs stuffed bandicoot.

**AVP.** Eye-height of prey vs cat. Gaze does not shut the gate.

---

### B. Flying-fox heat — January 2026 mortality + NSW heat-stress science

**Challenge.** Air ≥ ~42 °C kills grey-headed flying-foxes in Sydney camps; January 2026 was the worst mass die-off since Black Summer; juveniles die first.

**Non-human.** *Pteropus poliocephalus* (EPBC vulnerable). Pups/dependents first.

**IxD.** Feedforward from BoM **before** panting. Default off. Wolli Creek already used **track closure** as mitigation — a gate that closes to dogs/people on forecast heat is closer to documented practice than a tourist mister.

**Two honest builds (pick one):**

1. **Heat-box leaf + mist analog** (sprinkler papers; never a live camp).
2. **Heat-day camp-edge track latch** (NPWS already closed tracks 10 Jan 2026): lux + BoM + mmWave → physical barrier on a demo path.

**Architecture (2 is easier to defend).** ESP32-S3, DHT22, BH1750, WiFi BoM, MG90S latch, PETG “park furniture.”

**AVP.** Thermal volume / “who dies first (pups).” Headset never opens the latch.

---

### C. Koala road barrier — 110 species + Appin Rd 2026

**Challenge.** SW Sydney koala corridors are being cut by housing and roads; a $12m underpass has had **zero** koala records in five months while vehicle strike continues.

**Non-human.** *Phascolarctos cinereus* (Qld/NSW/ACT) — Action Plan priority.

**IxD.** Do **not** claim you fixed Appin Road. The documented failure is: animals do not enter a dark 52 m box. Design **underpass furniture** TfNSW already lists (escape hatches, one-way flaps, cover, moisture): a hatch that a koala-sized body can use and a dog cannot, with light/humidity that is not a glare trap (DCCEEW light guidelines: mammals).

**Architecture.** ESP32-S3, BH1750, soil moisture or DHT, LD2410, MG90S one-way hatch, timber/PETG tunnel analog ~1.5 m. Demo: stuffed koala vs dog vs person.

**AVP.** Inside-the-culvert darkness vs canopy continuity (why overpasses are argued).

---

### D. Swift parrot + STIF remnant — 110 species + Concord 7 Sep 2026

**Challenge.** Swift parrots are critically endangered; this month’s Inner West rezoning fight is about winter habitat, **noise and light**, and stormwater into endangered turpentine-ironbark forest.

**Non-human.** *Lathamus discolor*; the forest community as co-stakeholder.

**IxD.** Calm: work-light analog **defaults off** at dusk in “flowering season” (BoM + lux). Optional mic + BirdNET for parrot vs miner vs silence. Stormwater tray as second actuator (ties to habitat, not a second project).

**Architecture.** ESP32-S3, BH1750, I2S mic, MOSFET amber work-light (DCCEEW: amber/PC-amber), MG90S first-flush flap into a dirty tray. Enclosure: ironbark-coloured bollard that is **not** a path-light product (old topic stays dead).

**AVP.** Canopy as forage, not as real estate.

---

### E. *Posidonia* / mooring scour — endangered NSW seagrass, Feb 2026 SIMS

**Challenge.** *Posidonia australis* is endangered in all six NSW listed estuaries; Harbour meadows are shredded by swinging mooring chains, boats, construction, pollution; natural recovery is not expected.

**Non-human.** The seagrass meadow (habitat former for fish, including White’s seahorse — also on the 110).

**IxD.** Two moorings in a tank: chain that scours vs floating rode that does not. Turbidity + downwelling lux decide a servo that **lifts the chain analog**. You are staging SIMS’s EFM vs traditional mooring.

**Architecture.** 20 L tank, analog turbidity + BH1750 over the bed, ESP32-S3, MG90S, timber “seabed.” Electronics dry.

**AVP.** Light climate in the meadow (what the plant needs).

---

### F. Phytophthora / chytrid hygiene gate — two KTPs + Wollemi on the 110

**Challenge.** Walkers spread *Phytophthora cinnamomi* (KTP; Sydney sandstone, Royal NP) and amphibian chytrid (KTP). Commonwealth *Arrive Clean, Leave Clean* is the actual intervention, not a “smart pond.”

**Non-human.** Proteaceous plants / Wollemi (110) for Phytophthora; frogs for chytrid. Pick **one** pathogen in the report.

**IxD.** Human is the vector. Path does not open until wash-water turbidity/conductivity says the boot was rinsed. Ritual, not a dashboard.

**Architecture.** ESP32-S3, analog turbidity or TDS, BH1750 (day use), MG90S turnstile, PETG boot-bath. BoM rain: don’t freeze people in a storm without a documented override.

**AVP.** “What you would carry into the remnant.” Gaze does not unlock.

---

### G. Myrtle rust tool-lock — Action Plan 2026 target + novel-biota KTP

**Challenge.** Myrtle rust is under the novel-biota KTP; the Action Plan wants listed Myrtaceae secured in insurance collections by 2026. Sydney is inside the established range; moving dirty tools/plants spreads it.

**Non-human.** Local Myrtaceae (do not pretend you hold Wollemi). Native guava *Rhodomyrtus psidioides* is on the 110 and is myrtle-rust wrecked — name it as the listed plant, demo on a common *Callistemon*/lilly pilly analog.

**IxD.** Pruning-tool dock that **stays locked** until a wet-time + alcohol-wipe sensor (or timed dip) completes. Plant people, not an app.

**Architecture.** ESP32-S3, moisture/leaf-wetness, limit switch, MG90S lock, PETG dock.

### H. Cooks / Wolli first-flush — habitat-degradation mechanism + live catchment politics

**Challenge.** SoE: habitat degradation is how most threats land. Cooks River councils in 2025 still name **stormwater** as the major ongoing contaminant; EPA fined a 2024 untreated discharge to the Cooks.

**Non-human.** Macroinvertebrates / remaining fish (report: pollution-tolerant assemblages). Do not invent a listed eel if you cannot cite one for that reach.

**IxD.** Two trays; first N mm dirty. Water is the display.

**Architecture.** As previously sketched (rain + turbidity + MG90S weir). Now the citation is CRVA + EPA, not “we thought creeks were dirty.”

### I. Camp-edge light / disturbance — DCCEEW flying-fox camp guideline + Light Pollution Guidelines 2023 (bats appendix)

**Challenge.** National guidance: do not light nationally important flying-fox camps; disturbance during heat is worse. (This is **not** the old path-light bollard.)

**Non-human.** Grey-headed flying-fox at camp rest.

**IxD.** Default dark. If a demo “service light” exists, it is underside, warm, motion, off in heat-forecast. Combines with B2 (track latch).

**Do not** rebuild Wildlife-First Adaptive Path Light.
